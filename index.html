The Syriac Orthodox liturgy on your phone: the Beth Gazo with recordings of its melodies, the Shimo prayers of the hours, prayer books, the Peshitta Bible with translation and the church calendar – completely offline.

Die syrisch-orthodoxe Liturgie auf dem Handy: der Beth Gazo mit Aufnahmen seiner Melodien, das Stundengebet Shimo, Gebetsbücher, die Peshitta mit Übersetzung und der Kirchenkalender – vollständig offline.

Beth Gazo

The treasury of melodies with recordings – many Qole in up to eight tones.

Shimo

The prayers of the hours for every day of the week.

Prayer books

Husoyo, Fenqitho, Sunday hymns and the Tekso d'Qurobo in three text versions.

Peshitta

The Syriac Bible with German, English or Swedish translation.

Church year

Feasts, fasts and the Gospel readings of the Sundays.

Verse of the Day

A daily verse in the app, as a widget and as an optional reminder.

Sources & Thanks

Many texts in this app come from KtobeSuryoye.nl (liturgical texts) and Peshitta.eu (the Syriac Bible), the result of more than ten years of work. Thank you!
